# Nutrition guide

**The targets:** protein is set in grams first, about 0.7-1 g per lb of goal bodyweight (1.6-2.2 g/kg), a baseline that scales with the person, not a fixed number everyone converges on. Fat sits at 25-30% of calories and never drops below about 0.3 g per lb, enough to keep hormones and satiety in check without eating into the protein or carb budget. Carbs fill whatever's left, and that's where the two profiles below diverge. The ratios are starting points at typical calorie levels. As calories rise, hold protein grams steady and put the extra into carbs, so the percentages shift.

- **Recomp (lose fat, hold or build muscle): 45 / 25 / 30 C/F/P.** Calories sit at or slightly below maintenance. Carbs stay high enough to fuel training, protein stays high enough to protect muscle, and fat stays in the lower part of its range.
- **Clean bulk: 40 / 30 / 30 C/F/P.** A small calorie surplus, so there's more fat headroom. Protein grams hold steady and most of the extra calories go to carbs and fat.

Same skeleton, different dial. Protein is the one number that stays put in grams, and the percentages move around it as calories change.
