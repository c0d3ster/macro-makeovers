## 14. Chicken Parm Skillet | $$ · ~35 min · ●●○○○

Chicken parm without the pasta and the deep-fried breading. A thin parmesan crust keeps the crunch, spinach wilts into the marinara as the base, and the cheese on top is where the fat budget goes. **Makes 4 servings.**

### Nutrition

|                           | Calories | Carbs | Fat   | Protein | Ratio C/F/P    |
| ------------------------- | -------- | ----- | ----- | ------- | -------------- |
| **Per serving (÷4)**      | ~400     | 11g   | 17g   | 51g     | 11 / 38 / 51   |
| **With 1 oz dry pasta**   | ~510     | 33g   | 18g   | 55g     | 26 / 31 / 43   |
| **With 2 oz dry pasta**   | ~620     | 54g   | 19g   | 59g     | 35 / 27 / 38   |
| **Whole batch**           | ~1,600   | 44g   | 67g   | 205g    | -              |
| _Traditional per serving_ | _~1,050_ | _90g_ | _50g_ | _60g_   | _34 / 43 / 23_ |

The default is the low-carb-day build. Add pasta underneath on a day with more carbs to spend. The pasta rows scale the sauce with the pasta: 1.75 cups with 1 oz dry per serving (4 oz for the pan), and 2 cups with a full 2 oz dry per serving (8 oz, half a box). The traditional row is a typical restaurant plate with pasta. The sauce sets a lot of the fat here. Filippo Berio keeps it lean at 3.5g fat per 1/2 cup, and richer jars like Mezzetta and Rao's push the fat share to about 41 to 42% (see Sauce options).

### Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 1.5 lb chicken breast (4 breasts, about 6 oz each)
- 1 egg, beaten with 1 tbsp water
- 1/3 cup grated parmesan
- 1/4 cup panko
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Oil spray (avocado or olive), for the air fryer
- Salt, pepper

**Skillet**

- 10 oz baby spinach
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 cups Filippo Berio Marinara (made with Italian tomatoes). Use 1.75 cups with 1 oz pasta per serving and 2 cups with 2 oz
- 8 slices Galbani fresh mozzarella log, pre-sliced (2 per breast, about 1/2 oz each, 4 oz total)
- Fresh basil, to finish

**Optional pasta**

- 1 oz dry spaghetti or spaghetti rigati per serving (4 oz for the pan), or 2 oz per serving (8 oz, half a box)

### Method

Bake it in the oven on a rack (all 4 breasts at once) or in an air fryer (2 at a time). The skillet holds the sauce either way.

**Prep** (~10 min)

- Pound each breast to an even 1/2 to 3/4 inch. Pat very dry.
- Mix the parmesan, panko, and Italian seasoning on a plate. Beat the egg with the water in a shallow dish.
- If using pasta, boil it 1 to 2 min under the package time so it stays al dente, then drain.
- Mince the garlic. Take 8 slices from the pre-sliced mozzarella log and pat them dry with a paper towel.

**Cook** (~35 min in the oven, ~45 min in the air fryer)

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, dip in the egg, then press the parmesan mix on firmly on both sides. Let the breasts sit 5 min so the crust sets.
2. Cook the chicken, one of two ways:
   - **Oven:** heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the upper third. Set a wire rack on a sheet pan and spray it. Lay the breasts on the rack and spray both sides generously with oil. Bake 18 to 22 min, flipping once halfway, until golden and 165°F inside. No rack? Use parchment and flip the breasts halfway.
   - **Air fryer:** preheat to 400°F. Spray both sides of the breasts generously with oil. Air fry 2 breasts at a time, 12 to 16 min, flipping halfway, until golden and 165°F inside. Keep the first batch warm while the second cooks.
3. While the chicken cooks, warm a large skillet over medium. Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then the spinach in handfuls until wilted. Pour in the marinara and simmer 2 min, then keep it on low.
4. Lay 2 slices of mozzarella on each breast and cook until melted: 2 to 3 min in the oven (a minute under the broiler for color), or 1 to 2 min in the air fryer.
5. Spoon the spinach and sauce onto plates (or serve from the skillet) and set the breasts on top or alongside, never sauce over the crust. Finish with basil, and serve over the pasta if using.

### Make-or-break

- Pat the chicken **very dry** before the egg, or the crust slides off
- Even thickness. Pound the breasts so they cook through in the time it takes the crust to brown
- **Spray the crust.** Dry panko only browns with oil on it
- **Oven:** use a wire rack so air reaches the bottom. **Air fryer:** don't crowd the basket, two breasts at a time
- Sauce **under or beside** the chicken, not on it, so the crust stays crisp
- **Pat the fresh mozzarella dry.** It weeps water, which thins the sauce and softens the crust

### Fixes

- **Pale or dusty crust:** spray more oil, and make sure the panko was pressed on firmly
- **Crust browns before the chicken is cooked:** pound it thinner, or lower the heat 25°F and add a few minutes
- **Soggy bottom (oven):** the breasts were on a flat pan. Use a wire rack
- **Dry chicken:** pound thinner, and pull at 160°F, since it carries over
- **Watery sauce:** simmer 2 more minutes
- **Watery top:** blot the mozzarella slices before they go on

### Sauce options

Filippo Berio is the default. Per 1/2 cup, from retailer label listings, so check the jar. The pan ratios are for the no-pasta build with 1.5 cups of sauce.

| Sauce                          | Calories | Carbs | Fat  | Italian tomatoes              | Pan ratio C/F/P |
| ------------------------------ | -------- | ----- | ---- | ----------------------------- | --------------- |
| Filippo Berio (default)        | 60       | 6g    | 3.5g | Yes                           | 11 / 38 / 51    |
| Bertolli Organic               | 70       | 11g   | 1.5g | Not stated, likely US         | 15 / 34 / 51    |
| Rao's Homemade                 | 100      | 6g    | 7g   | Yes                           | 11 / 41 / 48    |
| Carbone Marinara               | 90       | 6g    | 7g   | Yes                           | 10 / 42 / 48    |
| Mezzetta Spicy                 | 100      | 9g    | 8g   | Mixed, Italian and California | 12 / 41 / 47    |

Mezzetta Spicy is the one with built-in heat. With a milder sauce, add chili flakes.

## 15. Garlic Sirloin Squash Stir Fry | $$ · ~30 min · ●●○○○

A takeout stir fry with the rice and the sugary sauce taken out. Lean sirloin, a big pile of mushrooms, zucchini, yellow squash, pepper, and onion, and a lot of garlic. The flavor comes from char and aromatics, so it never tastes like diet food. **Makes 4 servings.**

### Nutrition

|                           | Calories | Carbs | Fat   | Protein | Ratio C/F/P    |
| ------------------------- | -------- | ----- | ----- | ------- | -------------- |
| **Per serving (÷4)**      | ~405     | 26g   | 15g   | 43g     | 25 / 32 / 42   |
| **Whole batch**           | ~1,615   | 102g  | 58g   | 171g    | -              |
| _Traditional per serving_ | _~900_   | _95g_ | _40g_ | _40g_   | _42 / 40 / 18_ |

The traditional row is a takeout beef stir fry with white rice. The carbs here come from the vegetables and the sauce, with nothing underneath. See below for chicken options.

### Ingredients

**Stir fry**

- 1.5 lb top sirloin, trimmed
- 3 medium bell peppers (mix of red and green)
- 2 medium onions
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 2 medium zucchini
- 1 medium yellow squash
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 tsp avocado oil
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, to finish
- Chili flakes (optional)

**Sauce**

- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp honey
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp cornstarch, whisked with 1/4 cup water
- Black pepper

### Method

**Prep** (~15 min)

- Freeze the sirloin for 20 min, then slice it against the grain as thin as you can.
- Slice the mushrooms. Cut the peppers and onions into strips, and the zucchini and yellow squash into half-moons about 1/4 inch thick.
- Mince the garlic and split it into two piles.
- Whisk the sauce together, including the cornstarch slurry.

**Cook** (~15 min)

1. Get a wide skillet or wok very hot. Add 2 tsp of the avocado oil and sear the beef in two batches, about 1 to 2 min each, until browned. Move to a plate.
2. Add the remaining 2 tsp oil. Add the mushrooms in one layer and leave them alone for 2 min, then stir and cook 2 to 3 min more until browned and their liquid is gone.
3. Add the onions and peppers and cook 3 to 4 min until the edges char. Add half the garlic.
4. Add the zucchini and yellow squash and cook 2 to 3 min until just tender with some browned edges. They should still have a bite.
5. Return the beef, add the rest of the garlic, and pour in the sauce. Toss for about 1 min until glossy.
6. Off the heat, drizzle with the sesame oil, add chili flakes if using, and serve.

### Make-or-break

- Slice the beef **thin and against the grain**. Freezing it for 20 min makes this easy
- **Hot pan, small batches.** A crowded pan steams the squash into mush
- **Mushrooms get their own sear.** Give them a dry, hot layer and don't stir for the first 2 min, or they steam gray instead of browning
- Onions and peppers next, squash last. They need different times
- **Split the garlic.** Half cooks in, half goes in at the end for a sharper flavor
- Add salt at the end, not before. Salt pulls water out of the squash

### Fixes

- **Watery:** the pan was too crowded or too cool. Cook the vegetables in two batches next time
- **Tough beef:** sliced too thick or cooked too long. Sear hard and fast and pull it early
- **Sauce too thin:** simmer 30 more seconds, or add another splash of the slurry
- **Bland:** more garlic, more lime, a pinch of salt at the end

### Meat options (per serving)

| Meat                   | Calories | Carbs | Fat | Protein | Ratio C/F/P  |
| ---------------------- | -------- | ----- | --- | ------- | ------------ |
| Top sirloin (default)  | ~405     | 26g   | 15g | 43g     | 25 / 32 / 42 |
| Chicken breast         | ~390     | 26g   | 12g | 44g     | 27 / 28 / 45 |
| Boneless chicken thigh | ~400     | 26g   | 16g | 38g     | 26 / 36 / 38 |

Thighs are juicier and more forgiving, at the cost of a few grams of fat. Trim them well.

## 16. Greek Chicken Bowl | $$ · ~30 min + marinate · ●●○○○

A gyro plate without the pita and fries. Lemon-oregano chicken over crisp romaine with cucumber, tomato, olives, and feta, and a thick tzatziki made from nonfat Greek yogurt. Served cold and fresh, so it feels nothing like the hot, seared bowls. **Makes 4 servings.**

### Nutrition

|                                | Calories | Carbs | Fat   | Protein | Ratio C/F/P    |
| ------------------------------ | -------- | ----- | ----- | ------- | -------------- |
| **With chickpeas (÷4)**        | ~440     | 25g   | 15g   | 52g     | 22 / 30 / 48   |
| **No chickpeas**               | ~390     | 16g   | 14g   | 50g     | 16 / 32 / 51   |
| **Lamb leg instead of chicken**| ~485     | 25g   | 21g   | 51g     | 20 / 38 / 42   |
| **Whole batch (chickpeas)**    | ~1,760   | 98g   | 59g   | 209g    | -              |
| _Traditional gyro plate_       | _~970_   | _85g_ | _50g_ | _45g_   | _35 / 46 / 19_ |

The default has half a can of chickpeas for the beans and fiber, about 1/4 cup per bowl. Leave them out for the lowest-carb version. The traditional row is a typical gyro plate with pita, fries, and sauce.

### Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 1.5 lb chicken breast, cut into strips or left as cutlets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

**Tzatziki**

- 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Fresh dill or mint, salt

**Bowl**

- 2 romaine hearts, chopped
- 2 medium cucumbers, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 medium red onion, thinly sliced
- 3 oz feta, crumbled
- 1/4 cup kalamata olives, halved
- 1/2 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed

### Method

**Prep** (~15 min, plus at least 30 min to marinate)

- Toss the chicken with the oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Marinate at least 30 min, up to overnight.
- Make the tzatziki: squeeze the grated cucumber very dry, then stir into the yogurt with the garlic, lemon, dill, and salt. Chill.
- Chop the romaine, cucumbers, tomatoes, and onion.

**Cook** (~15 min)

1. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high. Cook the chicken 4 to 5 min per side until browned and 165°F inside. Rest 5 min, then slice.
2. Build the bowls: romaine, cucumber, tomato, onion, olives, and chickpeas, topped with the chicken, feta, and a big spoonful of tzatziki.

### Make-or-break

- **Squeeze the cucumber dry** for the tzatziki or it turns watery
- Marinate the chicken at least 30 min. Lemon and oregano do the work that fat usually does
- Slice the chicken **after** resting so the juices stay in
- Keep the tzatziki cold. It is a big part of why the bowl feels rich

### Fixes

- **Watery tzatziki:** more squeezing, or stir in a spoonful more yogurt
- **Dry chicken:** thinner cutlets, and pull at 160°F, since it carries over
- **Bland:** more lemon, more salt, more oregano on the chicken
- **Too sharp:** soak the sliced red onion in cold water for 5 min first

### Lamb option

Lean leg of lamb, trimmed and sliced thin, lands around 38% fat as written. Ground lamb is much fattier and pushes the bowl past 50%. I earlier suggested mixing 1 part ground lamb with 2 parts lean meat, but that works out closer to 41% fat, so leg of lamb is the better lamb swap. Store-bought gyro slices are typically a fatty beef and lamb blend and would also run well above 35%, so check the label before using them.
