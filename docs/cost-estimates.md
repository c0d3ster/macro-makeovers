## Summary

Prepared cost per serving runs from $2.74 (Blackened Chicken Alfredo) to $7.72 (Beef Bulgogi). Ratings follow the bands below. Every ingredient is priced at the lowest price found at a national chain (Walmart, Target, Costco, Kroger and its stores, and Albertsons and its stores such as Acme and Safeway) or on your local Martin's shelf and receipt. Regional chains such as H-E-B and ShopRite are listed only as comparison prices, since most shoppers can't reach them. Prices differ by region, so the lowest price keeps the ratings relative and useful for bargain shoppers. The price book names the store behind each price and lists the other prices found. Every item is priced from the smallest pack that covers what the recipe uses, not the cheapest bulk size, so a recipe using 4 oz of cheddar is costed from the 8 oz block. Cheddar is priced as Tillamook Extra Sharp White rather than store brand, and chicken breast is priced at your $3.00 per lb target, which the Martin's shelf supports (its Our Brand family pack is $2.49 per lb). ## Method and assumptions

Each ingredient is costed as pack price divided by pack quantity, times the amount the recipe uses. The batch total is the sum, and per serving is the batch divided by the recipe's serving count. Cost columns are rounded to the cent, so line items can differ from the batch total by 1 to 2 cents. The full-pack cost is what it takes to buy everything in the recipe from scratch, rounded up to whole packs, with exact weight for items sold per pound. It is split into shopping items and pantry staples. Pantry staples are oils, butter, milk, flour, cornstarch, brown sugar, soy sauce, Worcestershire, sesame oil and seeds, and every spice, since most kitchens already hold them. Every item is priced at a small pack close to the recipe amount, so neither the prorated nor the full-pack figures lean on bulk sizes.

- **Leftover components are prorated by use.** The taco pico (4/7 of a batch), the pollo asado salsa (4/5 of a batch) and the cottage cheese blend (5/6 of a batch in the chili, 2/3 in the tacos) are costed only for the share the recipe consumes.
- **Spices are costed per teaspoon.** Jar price divided by the teaspoons in the jar, using ounces per jar and grams per teaspoon.
- **Each item uses the smallest pack that covers the recipe.** The goal is fewer leftovers for someone cooking one recipe, balanced against price: the bulk size is cheaper per ounce but leaves more unused. The price is that pack's lowest price found, prorated by use. A larger pack is only priced in when the recipe needs more than the smaller one holds, or, in the basket, when pooled recipes need more. A bigger pack still wins when it costs at most 10% more than the smaller one, since a few cents more for twice the product is the better buy. That keeps the 17 fl oz olive oil, the 16 oz bacon and the 3 lb potato bag. Smaller packs in use: the 8 oz cheddar block, the 2 lb flour bag, 8 oz sour cream, 5.3 oz cottage cheese cups (and the 2-cup pack), 6 eggs, 8 oz of butter, 5 oz grated parmesan, an 8 oz Oaxaca ball, 2 oz of pine nuts, 14.5 oz broth cans and 16 oz vinegar. A smaller pack that costs more than the bigger one is never used: spinach, pickles, Worcestershire, brown sugar, sugar, saltines and tomato paste keep their larger packs.
- **Whole items are priced each and prorated by the fraction used.** Half an onion costs half the price of one.
- **Each ingredient uses the lowest price found at a national chain or Martin's.** Prices come from the Martin's shelf at the Winchester, VA store (read October 29), your Martin's receipt, and Walmart, Target, Kroger, Albertsons (Acme) and Costco listings. H-E-B and ShopRite prices stay in the price book as comparisons only. The price book shows the winning store and the other prices found for every item that has more than one. Treat the totals as relative costs, since prices vary by region and store.
- **Pack sizes and approximations.** Oils are priced at 8.4 fl oz avocado and 17 fl oz olive (the 8.5 fl oz olive oil is within 10% of it). Cheddar is Tillamook Extra Sharp White in the 8 oz block, not store brand. Chicken breast is your $3.00 per lb target. The baby potato bag is assumed to be 1.5 lb.
- **Not costed.** Salt and water.
