## Canceling

Cancel from your account through the billing portal at any time. There are no cancellation fees. You keep paid features until the end of your current billing month, then you drop back to the free plan. Your account, recipes, and basket stay.

## Refunds

Subscriptions are billed monthly, and we don't give partial refunds for the unused part of a month after you cancel.

We will refund your most recent charge if you email us within **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: 7]** days of it and:

- you were charged after canceling, or charged twice,
- you meant to cancel and haven't used the paid features since the charge, or
- a paid feature didn't work for you and we couldn't fix it.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually show up in 5 to 10 business days. Where local law gives you more rights than this policy, the law wins.

## Groceries

Groceries you order after "Send to cart" are bought from the grocery service, not from us. Missing items, substitutions, delivery problems, and grocery refunds are handled by that service under its own policy.
