## What we collect

- **Your account:** your email, plus your name and photo if you sign in with Google. Passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider.
- **What you save:** your basket and any plans or profile details you add.
- **Billing:** your plan and billing dates. Card details go to Stripe, never to us.
- **Usage:** page views and a few events like sign-ups, through Vercel Analytics, which uses no cookies.

We don't track what you eat, and we don't sell your data or use it for ads.

## Services we use

Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting and analytics), Resend (email), Cloudflare R2 (recipe photos), and Stripe (payments). They only handle data to run the site.

## Grocery partners

"Send to cart" sends your basket items to the grocer you pick, like Instacart, Kroger, or Walmart. Their privacy policy covers what happens there. Some are affiliate links, so we may earn a commission.

## Cookies

Only what the site needs: keeping you signed in and remembering your basket and theme.

## Your data
