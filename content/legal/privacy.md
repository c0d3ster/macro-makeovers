## What we collect

- **Your account.** Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares. If you sign up with email, your password is stored hashed by our auth provider, and we never see it.
- **Your subscription, once billing launches.** Whether you are subscribed, your plan, and billing dates. Card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us.
- **What you save.** Your basket lives in your browser until you sign in. Features like saved baskets, meal plans, or profile details (for example a goal weight) are stored with your account when you use them.
- **Messages you send.** If you email us or use the contact form, we keep your name, email, and message so we can reply.
- **Basic usage.** Page views and a few product events (like sign-ups and checkouts) so we know what is working. See analytics below.

We do not track what you eat or your daily intake, and we do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## Who helps us run the site

These services process data for us, only to run the site:

- **Supabase:** database and sign-in. Holds your account and saved data.
- **Google:** Sign in with Google, if you choose it.
- **Vercel:** hosting and analytics. Sees requests to the site and page views.
- **Resend:** sending email. Sees your email address and the message.
- **Cloudflare R2:** serving recipe photos. Sees requests for images.
- **Stripe:** payments, once billing launches. Holds your payment details and billing email.

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Analytics, which counts page views and events without cookies and without building a profile of you across sites.

## Grocery partners and affiliate links

When you use "Send to cart", we send your basket's items to the grocer you pick (for example Instacart, Kroger, or Walmart) and open their site. What happens there, including your order, payment, and any cookies they set, is covered by that grocer's own privacy policy. Some of these links are affiliate links, so we may earn a commission on an order. We do not receive your payment details or order history from them.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use a few cookies and browser storage entries that the site needs: keeping you signed in, remembering your basket, and remembering your theme. No advertising cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except billing records we must keep for tax and accounting, and backups that roll off on their own schedule.
