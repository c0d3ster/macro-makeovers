## Why your numbers will differ

Carbs, fat, protein, and calories are worked out per serving from typical ingredient data. Brands, cuts, portion sizes, cooking, and swaps all change them, so check the labels on what you buy. The traditional row in each table is a typical version of the dish, not a specific brand or restaurant.

## Not medical advice

The targets in the guides are general ranges for healthy adults. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing how you eat, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating.

## Allergies and food safety

Recipes can contain common allergens, so read every label if you have an allergy. Cook meat, fish, and eggs to safe temperatures.
